Audio Engineering

Acoustics Tuned to Portable Speaker Physics

No artificial EQ bloat. Engineered specifically for small 1.5–2 inch Bluetooth drivers and clean dialogue.

🛡️

48 dB/oct Brickwall Voice Shield

Cascaded 8th-order Linkwitz-Riley crossover attenuates vocal fundamentals by >39 dB at 150 Hz, completely eliminating dialogue doubling and echo on Netflix and YouTube.

🚫

45 Hz 4th-Order Cone Protection

Crushes inaudible 20–35 Hz sub-flutter by up to 28 dB. Stops small speaker cones from bottoming out, flapping, or making loose paper rumbling noises on movie explosions.

🥊

75 Hz Tight Punch & Safe Headroom

Refined +1.8 dB punch at 75 Hz delivers tactile bass impact while a dedicated -1.1 dBFS limiter ceiling prevents portable speaker amplifier clipping and distortion.

⏱️

0–500ms Delay Compensation

Bluetooth transmission naturally incurs 100–250ms of audio delay. BassBridge's circular ring buffer delays the laptop speakers so both audio sources hit your ears in perfect sync.

🔊

Coherent Mono-Summed Bass

Subwoofers and portable speakers are single mono drivers. BassBridge mono-sums the sub-bass (<85 Hz) to eliminate stereo phase cancellation, doubling acoustic efficiency.

🎚️

Dynamic Soft-Knee Limiter

Interpolated sample-level envelope limiting prevents 46.9 Hz buffer clicks. Smooth analog-style soft saturation protects against harsh digital clipping.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

Does this work with an Amazon Echo Dot or boAt Bluetooth speaker? +
Yes! BassBridge works with any standard Bluetooth speaker, including Amazon Echo Dot (3rd, 4th, 5th Gen), boAt Stone, JBL Flip/Charge, Bose, Sony, and portable soundbars.
How does BassBridge eliminate Bluetooth audio delay? +
Bluetooth audio naturally incurs around 100ms to 200ms of transmission latency. BassBridge includes an adjustable Bass Delay slider (0 to 500 ms) that holds the laptop speaker audio in an internal circular buffer so both speakers hit your ears in exact synchronization.
Does it capture audio from YouTube, Spotify, and games? +
Yes. On Windows, BassBridge utilizes WASAPI loopback capture at the hardware endpoint level. Any sound played by any browser, music player, media app, or game is captured and split seamlessly.
What is Linkwitz-Riley LR4 and why does it matter? +
Standard audio filters often create a +3 dB phase bump at the crossover frequency, causing distortion when two speakers play together. Linkwitz-Riley 4th-order filters have a steep 24 dB/octave slope and sum completely flat (0 dB ripple) with zero phase cancellation at the crossover frequency.
Is BassBridge free and open-source? +
Yes, 100% free and open-source under the MIT license. You can inspect the source code, contribute, or build it yourself on GitHub.
