Engineered to eliminate Netflix/YouTube vocal doubling and small driver flapping.

🗣️

Vocal Presence Clarity (+2.0 dB)

Dolby-tuned vocal intelligibility filter at 2.4 kHz ensures speech and whispers on Netflix and YouTube cut through clearly without being drowned out by background music or heavy action scenes.

🛡️

LR8 48 dB/Octave Voice Isolation

Ordinary crossovers let 10%–20% of male vocal fundamentals bleed into the bass speaker. Our 8th-order Linkwitz-Riley filter delivers >39 dB attenuation at 150 Hz, eliminating voice doubling completely.

🛡️

55 Hz 8th-Order Rumble Elimination

Steep 48 dB/octave highpass filter suppresses 20–35 Hz sub-flutter by up to -70.3 dB. Completely eliminates driver over-excursion and "paper rumbling/flapping" on small Bluetooth speakers.

🎚️

Dynamic Auto-EQ & Bass De-Boomer

Automatically regulates device and bass volume based on frequency. When excessive bass or loud explosions hit, it ducks unnecessary bass to protect drivers and prioritizes dialogue clarity.

🔊

Psychoacoustic Virtual Subwoofer

Small 1.5–2" speakers cannot reproduce 30 Hz physically. Our algorithm synthesizes coherent 2nd harmonics so your ears perceive deep cinema sub-bass without pushing the driver past its physical excursion limits.

🌌

Dolby 3D Spatial Soundstage

Expands the stereo ambience and environmental immersion on your laptop or TV speakers while keeping the center dialog firmly anchored in the middle of your screen.

🎛️

Coherent Mono Summing

Sums left and right sub-bass into a single phase-locked mono channel before transmission, preventing acoustic cancellation in portable single-box Bluetooth speakers.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use BassBridge on my Smart TV without a laptop? +
Open your Smart TV browser (Amazon Silk on Fire TV, Samsung Internet, WebOS Browser on LG TV, or Chrome on Google TV) and visit https://2420090149-klh.github.io/BassBridge/. Bookmark it or pin it to your TV home screen. The real-time Web Audio engine runs directly on your TV! Alternatively, you can sideload the Android TV APK.
How does BassBridge eliminate Bluetooth audio delay? +
Bluetooth audio naturally incurs around 100ms to 200ms of transmission latency. BassBridge includes an adjustable Bass Delay slider (0 to 500 ms) with presets for Amazon Echo Dot (160ms), boAt Stone (140ms), and Smart TVs (180ms) that buffers audio so both speakers hit your ears in exact synchronization.
Why was voice echoing from my Bluetooth speaker, and is it fixed? +
Standard crossovers let lower male vocal frequencies (85–180 Hz) bleed into the subwoofer path, causing dialogue doubling. BassBridge now employs an 8th-Order Linkwitz-Riley (48 dB/octave) crossover at 85 Hz that suppresses voices by >39 dB, rendering the bass speaker 100% silent during dialogue.
What causes the "paper rumbling" sound on portable Bluetooth speakers? +
Portable Bluetooth speakers (like boAt Stone 1010 or Amazon Echo Dot) have 1.5–2 inch drivers that mechanically bottom out when fed inaudible 20–35 Hz frequencies. Our new 4th-Order 45 Hz High-Pass Subsonic Filter cuts this rumble by -28.2 dB, while synthesizing psychoacoustic 2nd harmonics so you hear deep, clean bass without driver distortion.
Is BassBridge completely free? +
Yes, 100% free and open-source under the MIT license. You can inspect the source code, contribute, or build it yourself on GitHub.
